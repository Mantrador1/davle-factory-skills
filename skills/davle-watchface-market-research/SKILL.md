# DAVLE Watch Face Market Research

## Objective
Produce a fresh, evidence-backed market snapshot for paid Wear OS watch faces. Do not rely on stale embedded conclusions when current research is possible.

## Workflow
1. Search current Google Play and reputable secondary sources for paid/successful Wear OS watch faces.
2. Build a comparison set of at least 20 relevant products when the market provides enough evidence.
3. Capture, when available: product name, developer, price, rating, review volume, install/download signal, update recency, style, analog/digital/hybrid class, visible complications, customization claims, AOD claims, and store imagery cues.
4. Separate observed facts from inference.
5. Flag missing or unverifiable metrics instead of guessing.
6. Prefer recent evidence and note the research date.

## Evidence rules
- Never call something a bestseller solely because it looks popular.
- Do not infer sales from review count without labeling the inference.
- Distinguish paid ranking, popularity, rating, and install count.
- Treat screenshots and store copy as product-positioning evidence, not proof of conversion.
- Preserve source links or source identifiers in the research output.

## Output
Return a structured market table plus:
- strongest current commercial clusters,
- notable outliers,
- repeated buyer-facing promises,
- data gaps,
- concise handoff to `davle-watchface-trend-analysis`.

## Quality gate
Reject the research as insufficient if it is based on fewer than 10 credible current references without explicitly stating the limitation.
