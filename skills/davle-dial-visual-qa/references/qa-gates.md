# DAVLE Dial Visual QA Gates

## Gate order
Evaluate hard gates before aesthetic scoring. Never average away a critical failure.

## G1 — Dial-only
PASS only when the image contains dial artwork without physical watch case, crown, lugs, buttons, strap/bracelet, wrist/hand, product scene, or packaging.

## G2 — Front-facing orthographic view
PASS only when the dial is visually straight-on and not materially distorted by perspective or tilt.

## G3 — Circular geometry
Check:
- circular silhouette,
- centered visual mass,
- consistent radial spacing where intended,
- uniform index intervals where intended,
- one coherent center pivot for analog hands,
- no visibly warped rings.

## G4 — Primary readability
At a glance, the intended primary time must be identifiable before secondary data. Fail if hands/digits merge with decoration or if the time is materially ambiguous.

## G5 — Text/numeral integrity
Critical functional numerals must be correctly formed and non-duplicated. Nonessential microtext artifacts are defects; severe pseudo-text noise is a revision trigger.

## G6 — Controlled density
Secondary data must form deliberate zones. Fail or revise when unrelated elements compete equally, touch/overlap, or create accidental visual noise.

## G7 — Commercial thumbnail test
Mentally reduce the dial to small Play Store screenshot/thumbnail size. Confirm that the primary visual hook, time hierarchy, and distinctive silhouette remain legible. A concept that works only when enlarged should not receive maximum commercial-appeal score.

## G8 — Concept consistency
Compare against the approved commercial thesis. Do not accept an attractive image that abandons the chosen target, hierarchy, or differentiation strategy.

## G9 — Originality
Reject recognizable competitor branding, logos, signature identity, or near-copy composition. Abstract market patterns are allowed; distinctive competitor expression is not.

## G10 — Factory-reference suitability
PASS when the image can reasonably guide implementation: clear zones, coherent geometry, separable elements, intentional empty space, and no essential effect that depends on impossible physical reflections or perspective.

## Defect severity
- CRITICAL: violates dial-only, originality, core geometry, or primary readability. Cannot ACCEPT.
- MAJOR: meaningfully harms commercial or implementation quality. Requires REVISE unless explicitly tolerated.
- MINOR: polish defect that does not block implementation or comprehension.

## Regeneration instruction format
For each CRITICAL/MAJOR defect specify:
1. observed defect,
2. element to preserve,
3. exact change,
4. forbidden recurrence.

Example:
Observed: inconsistent radial index spacing from 4–7 o'clock.
Preserve: primary digital time and cyan accent ring.
Change: rebuild all twelve major indices at uniform 30-degree intervals with identical length.
Avoid: no extra decorative markers between major indices.
